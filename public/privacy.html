Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bianca Elena Gaman, licensed insurance agent ("we", "us"), collects and uses information when you visit lifeinsurancebianca.com or request a quote.

Information we collect

When you fill out the quote form, we collect the information you provide:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Age
  • The type of life insurance you are interested in
  • Any questions or comments you choose to include

Our hosting provider may also automatically record basic technical data, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, to keep the site running and secure.

How we use your information

  • To contact you by phone, email or text message about your quote request
  • To prepare insurance quotes and recommend coverage options
  • To answer your questions and provide ongoing service
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With insurance carriers, when needed to obtain a quote or apply for coverage on your behalf
  • With service providers that help us run this website, such as our form provider (Formspree) and hosting provider (Vercel)
  • When required by law or to protect our legal rights

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as needed to respond to your request, provide service, and meet record-keeping requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your information, or to stop contacting you. Just email or call us using the details below.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information. The website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to protect the data you share.

Children

This website is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
